FAQ

The questions people usually ask before they say yes.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is scoped around its pages, features, content, and technical requirements. You'll receive a clear quote before work begins.

How long does it take?

A straightforward business site can often be completed in a few weeks, depending on content, revisions, and functionality.

Do you have formal credentials?

905 Online is an independent, self-taught web development practice. The work is the proof: you'll see the design and development before deciding.

Will my site work on phones?

Yes. Responsive, mobile-first behavior is part of the build rather than an optional extra.

Will you get me to #1 on Google?

No honest developer can guarantee that. The site can be built with strong technical and local SEO foundations, but rankings depend on competition, authority, content, relevance, and ongoing work.

Do I have to pay every month?

The website development can be a project fee. Domain, hosting, and optional maintenance are separate costs, and will be explained upfront.

What do you need from me?

Business information, services, photos, branding, contact details, and any examples or competitors you like. I'll guide you through the rest.

Can I request changes?

Yes. Revisions are included according to the project scope. Ongoing changes can be handled separately or through a maintenance plan.
