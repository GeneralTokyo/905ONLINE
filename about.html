Our story

My dream began with an interest in how things get built. Soon, it became a very real practice built on doing right by small businesses.

Where it started

Years of self-directed learning in design and development, spent figuring out how the web actually works — not just how it looks.

Why local businesses

Local businesses are often sold on flashy templates or vague promises. They deserve a website built around how their customers actually decide.

How it runs today

Every project is handled directly — no hand-offs, no account managers, no outsourced design or development.

Where it's headed

Turning consistent, honest work into a long-term career built on client results, not just finished sites.

FAQ

Questions people ask about working with an independent developer.

The primary focus is GTA and Ontario businesses, but remote projects can be discussed depending on scope.

What kind of businesses do you usually work with?

Local service businesses — contractors, hospitality, home services, and similar — that need a credible, functional website.
