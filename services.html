FAQ

Questions people usually ask about services & pricing.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is scoped around its pages, features, content, and technical requirements. You'll receive a clear quote before work begins.

How long does a project take?

A straightforward business site can often be completed in a few weeks, depending on content, revisions, and functionality.

Do I need the maintenance plan?

No. It's optional. Some clients prefer to request changes as needed; others prefer ongoing coverage. Either works.

Do you handle hosting and domains?

Yes. We can guide you through purchasing a domain and hosting, or work with an existing setup. We want you to own your online presence, so we'll make sure you do it step by step to understand what you're working with.

Will my site work well on phones?

Yes — every project is built mobile-first, rather than being adapted afterward.

Can you improve my existing website instead of a full rebuild?

Depending on the current site, sometimes. We can discuss whether a redesign or a rebuild makes more sense for your situation.
